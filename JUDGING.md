# A connected judging walkthrough

Use `http://localhost:3000`, local payment/email modes and the separate worker. All demonstration actions persist. Initial demo password: `Champions2026!`; addresses use `@champions.local`. `member`, `new`, `junior`, `expired` are customers; `reception`, `cashier`, `kitchen`, `owner` have their named roles. Seeds preserve existing actions. Reception, cashier and kitchen also have seeded salary records.

1. **Discover and enquire.** Explore all four photographic sport selectors by keyboard/touch, then submit a local enquiry. Reception opens Enquiries & CRM, assigns it, saves a note/plan quote and links the account. Conversion alone does not create a paid membership. New invitees use verification/reset links from the owner's Local test inbox.
2. **Become a member.** Customer opens Memberships or reception processes the reviewed plan. Confirm the explicitly simulated local payment. In My Account, show the plan card, validity, unique Champions ID, QR, history and itemized receipt. Renewal extends the term; old prices/benefits remain saved.
3. **Book and arrive.** Open Book a Court, select a sport/date/hour and review the server-priced hold. Confirm before expiry. Reception identifies the QR/manual Champions ID and performs single-use check-in within its allowed window. Friday social places and waiting offers use the same checkout and daily quota.
4. **Shop.** Select a real product variant, add to the persisted cart, choose pickup or delivery and confirm local checkout. Customer history shows the order/receipt. Cashier Inventory collects the pickup or dispatches/delivers the delivery, and can record a reasoned partial return/restock.
5. **Eat and settle.** Cashier opens an available table for the member, adds quantities and item notes, and submits the ticket. Kitchen accepts → cooks → marks ready; cashier marks served. Add another item to show an amendment/new ticket. Enable an eligible member tab, record partial/full settlement and close only after service and balance checks pass. Account, kitchen and billing show the same persisted records.
6. **Reconcile.** Owner opens Reports, applies Today/This week/This month/custom dates, then clicks a departmental figure or payment method. Sum the supporting records and open their invoices. Export the ledger CSV. Cashier opens a cash shift before recording **actual manually attested** cash in the local demo, records a reasoned payout, then closes with counted cash. Explain any discrepancy; inspect receipts/refunds/payouts. Local simulated payments never count as physical cash. Existing unassigned cash is reported separately.
7. **People and audit.** Owner opens People & payroll, schedules/cancels a shift with a reason, reviews a leave request and finalizes one demo payslip after checking salary/tax settings. Print it and export the configurable withholding summary. Staff can see only their own employment records. Owner searches the audit history by record ID/action.
8. **Reminders.** Owner opens Reminders & delivery to show scheduled 7/1/0-day messages, renewal links, mode/status, attempts and retry state. Timings are in Business settings (default 09:00 Asia/Kolkata). The worker schedules existing terms within one minute. Local messages appear in Local test inbox when due. Renewal suppresses earlier-term messages. The test suite demonstrates due-time processing immediately without changing a real member's expiry or the host clock.
9. **Prove races.** With the worker paused, run `npm run test:integration`. It issues competing same-court, daily-quota, last-social-place and last-online/counter-SKU requests against PostgreSQL. Exactly one scarce-resource request succeeds; failed carts roll back; identical checkout retries create one payment. The added connected journey test follows enquiry through owner reporting. Restart the worker afterward.
10. **State the boundaries.** Local receipts move no money. SMTP/Razorpay need credentials; missing integrations do not claim delivery or gateway success. Signature/capture/retry/exceptional repayment checks use a mocked provider, not live funds. Docker/remote CI/camera/physical LAN acceptance remains explicitly unverified in REQUIREMENTS.md.

For a saved owner example, Business documents contains the persisted **Stage 3 Local Demo Business** ₹1,000 court package and its simulated settled invoice. Cash reconciliation has a balanced zero-float/count shift. People & payroll has Neha's September 2026 ₹28,000/0% withholding demo payslip. These are illustrative local records, with no real client contract, payment or salary disbursement.
