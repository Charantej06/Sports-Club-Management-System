## Deliberate limits

Verification on this host: ESLint, TypeScript, four unit tests, 43 real-PostgreSQL/HTTP integration tests and production build passed; seven migrations are up to date and health is `ok`. Browser checks covered the approved customer destinations and owner/staff workspaces, keyboard interactions, desktop and 390px mobile layouts. Restart persistence was checked with the saved business invoice; its simulated settlement agrees with owner collections and outstanding. Cash closing and a printable finalized demo payslip were exercised. Screenshots and build/integration logs are in `.local`.

- SMTP and Razorpay credentials are absent. Local inbox delivery is proven; external SMTP/provider acceptance is not. `DELIVERED` on a local message means delivered to the database test inbox, never sent externally. Missing SMTP configuration produces retries/failed status.
- SMTP is at-least-once across a crash after provider acceptance and before database commit. Stable Message-ID and serialized workers reduce duplicates, but SMTP cannot promise provider exactly-once acceptance. Local processing is repeat-safe.
- Real gateway repayments are performed in the provider dashboard before recording evidence. Ordinary invoice refunds stay pending until staff records actual repayment with a reason. Exceptional unallocated captures require a processed full refund fetched from the provider before the ledger marks repayment; split exceptional repayments are not supported.
- Payroll withholding is an owner-configured calculation, not a statutory tax filing engine. Finalized payslips are calculation snapshots; bank salary disbursement is outside this application. Leave adjustments are explicit owner entries, not automatic salary deductions.
- Utilization uses current opening-hour policy and current closure records; historical opening-hour policy changes are not reconstructed. Financial invoice/benefit/payroll snapshots remain immutable.
- Safe item drafts retain no payment confirmation, reservation or guaranteed availability. Staff must review the current table/member after reconnecting. No service worker promises a cold-start UI on a fully disconnected device.
- No problem PDF was supplied. No physical camera, real mobile/LAN device, Docker runtime, external delivery/payment or remote CI acceptance is claimed.

The application is ready for local judging after the documented checks. Full deployment acceptance remains open until the external and hardware checks above are performed.
